## 1. Positioning Statement (the "one line")

> **"I help engineering teams recover complex e-commerce platforms and evolve them toward automated, cloud-native systems — with a growing focus on GCP and DataOps."**

This is the North Star. Every headline, bio, and post should ladder up to it.

### Why this works
- **Credible today**: matches what's been shipped (Daka, Biomercados, Crazy Imagine).
- **Honest about the trajectory**: GCP/DataOps is in progress, not claimed as already done.
- **Differentiated**: not many engineers combine *e-commerce rescue + platform recovery* + *DevOps* + a serious move into *GCP DataOps*. That's the angle to lean on.

## 5. Personal Website (`alvarezivan.net`)

Structure as a **portfolio of rescues**, not a resume.

```
/                 → Hero: positioning statement + photo + 1 CTA ("See case studies")
/case-studies/    → 4–6 deep dives (one per major rescue/build)
/now/             → "What I'm learning & shipping this quarter" (study plan in public)
/writing/         → Mirror of LinkedIn long-form posts (own the content)
/about/           → Story + timeline + certs
/contact/         → Email, LinkedIn, Calendly
```

### Case-study template
- **Context** (1 paragraph: what was broken)
- **Constraints** (team size, stack, deadline)
- **Architecture decision** (with a diagram — even hand-drawn)
- **Outcome** (numbers: users, uptime, deploy frequency, cost)
- **What I'd do differently on GCP today** ← bridges past to future

### Visual identity (cheap & consistent)
- **One accent color** (suggestion: deep teal or terminal-green — engineer-coded, not corporate-blue).
- **Mono font for code/headings, sans for body.** (JetBrains Mono + Inter.)
- **One consistent headshot** across LinkedIn, GitHub, site, conference bios.

## 6. Content Cadence (sustainable)

| Frequency | Format | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 2×/week | Short LinkedIn post (200–400 chars) — a lesson from today's work | LinkedIn |
| 1×/week | Carousel or "build log" from study plan | LinkedIn |
| 1×/month | Long-form case study | Site + LinkedIn article |
| 1×/quarter | Update `/now` page + a "what I learned" retrospective | Site |

**Content engine trick:** every Coursera module finished = 1 post ("Today I learned X, here's how I'd apply it to a Daka-style problem"). The study plan *is* the content calendar.

## 7. Visibility Tactics (multipliers)

1. **Build in public.** Push DevOps + GCP labs to GitHub with READMEs that read like case studies. Recruiters do look.
2. **Comment strategically.** 15 min/day commenting thoughtfully on posts by GCP, NestJS, Laravel thought leaders. Grows reach faster than posting.
3. **Open-source one tool.** Even small — e.g., a Laravel→BigQuery sync package, or a Filament SAP connector. One repo with stars = instant credibility.
4. **Speak.** Submit to local meetups (Caracas/remote LATAM tech) on "Rescuing Legacy E-commerce" — a topic owned.
5. **Bilingual advantage.** Post some content in Spanish. The LATAM senior-engineer market is underserved and pays USD.

## 9. What to *stop* doing

- Listing every framework in the headline. Reads as generic and doesn't differentiate.
- Generic "passionate developer" language in the summary.
- Posting tutorials that 10,000 others have written. Lean on **decisions and trade-offs** instead — that's the harder thing to copy.
